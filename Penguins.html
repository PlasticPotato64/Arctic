Fun facts about penguins

Penguins are excellent swimmers and can swim for long distances.

Some penguins like the Galapagos penguin live in much warmer climates near the equator.

Depending on the species, penguins spend up to 75% of their lives foraging for food in the ocean, eating fish, squid, and krill.
